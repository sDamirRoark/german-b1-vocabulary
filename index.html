How the review works

Again returns a word soon. Hard schedules a short review. Good and Easy gradually increase the interval. Smart review prioritizes words that are due, then introduces new words.

German entries and example sentences come from the uploaded Goethe-Zertifikat B1 vocabulary-list extraction. English meanings are supplemental study glosses, not official Goethe translations. The source extraction contains a few line-break/OCR artefacts, so keep the original PDF as the reference when an entry looks unusual.
